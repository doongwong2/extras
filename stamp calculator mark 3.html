How to Use

This calculator predicts the exact day you will finish your grind based on game bonus logic.

1. Current Stamps

Enter your exact count right now. Do not "backtrack" to the start of the event.

2. Extra Grind Rounds

This is how many extra games you play per day after getting your first-round bonuses. If you only play 1 round a day, keep this at 0.

3. Dates

The "Start Date" should be today. The "Deadline" is when the rewards disappear.

4. Monday Bonus (+4)

The tool automatically detects Mondays in the calendar and applies the Weekly Reset bonus at 07:00 AM.

5. Overlapping Bonuses

The tool assumes that on your first game of the day, you get the Base (1) + Daily (2). If it's Monday, you get an extra +4.
